We are happy to share that former CDNLab undergraduate research assistant Harshith Peta has accepted a job offer from Egen, a Chicago based technology company, as a full-stack engineer.
His role will focus on building healthcare-focused applications, developing backend services and user interfaces, and improving the observability, performance, and reliability of AI agents. 
Harshith was a research assistant from 2025 to 2026, and helped develop some of the lab's code infrastructure for natural language processing analysis.
